## Universal lessons (freezemag-base)
One line each, newest last, dated. Written by /freezemag-base:learn when a
correction applies to every repo; promoted from repo lessons by
/freezemag-base:retro. Keep it under 40 lines: a lesson that stops recurring
can go.

- 2026-09-02 A rule stated in prose recurs; a rule in a hook or a guard line does not. When a correction can be a guard, make it one.
- 2026-09-02 Cloud sessions never read ~/.claude. Anything meant for every session goes in this plugin, not in a home-directory file.
- 2026-09-02 Ad-hoc screenshots of the state just changed miss the defects. Sweep every mode, ground and viewport, and the exported files re-opened.
- 2026-09-02 CLAUDE.md grows because every rule said add to it and nothing said remove. The plugin now warns past 400 lines; decisions, history and area rules live in docs/ and .claude/rules/, and lessons in lessons files.
- 2026-09-04 A PR's checks say nothing about its state. Cem merged atlas #74 eighteen minutes after it opened; eight commits went to the same branch over four hours and were in no PR. Read the PR's state before every push; a merged PR is finished.
- 2026-09-05 Told Cem no BWT integration existed, then a Git pull add-on and an ESPHome preset would work, all from memory, and was wrong twice and corrected once. Never argue from memory and never assume what code does: read the vendor page, the integration source or the repo before stating what exists or how it behaves, and say what was read.
- 2026-09-05 A review finding that changes what the room sees is a design decision, and the operator makes it. A reviewer's contrast worry became a halo around every region name hours after Cem had the plate removed for being a background; fix a finding invisibly or ask, never by adding a mark.
- 2026-09-05 Every review fix is re-tested against the operator's own sentence in the operator's own state. The pulse was moved to the halos to satisfy a reviewer's rule, and halos do not exist at the reading zoom where Cem had asked for it, so nothing pulsed; the builder reported "open cards carry nothing" as a success.
- 2026-09-05 Two adversarial reviews and two screenshot audits, and the operator found four defects in five minutes: the audits looked at survey zoom on black, he looks at reading zoom on white. Before shipping, reproduce the state in the operator's last screenshots (ground, zoom, what is lit) and look at that.
- 2026-09-06 Pushed four commits to a smarthome branch whose PR had been closed and fast-forwarded into main; the prepush-merged guard exempts that shape and the prose rule failed twice. A push to a non-default branch now holds the session until a PR is created or subscribed to (postpush-pr guard); a rule that can be a hook is a hook.
